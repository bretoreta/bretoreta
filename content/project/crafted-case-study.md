# Case Study: CraftEd – University Placement Website for Global Education Pathways to Malaysia

## Client: CraftEd — The Craft Catalyst (Nairobi, Kenya & Shah Alam, Malaysia)

CraftEd is the signature university placement solution of The Craft Catalyst, a **CUE-approved University Agent in Kenya** recognized by the Commission for University Education. CraftEd supports students and families from across East Africa — from the very first consultation to successful university placement and settling into life in Malaysia. The service provides guidance that is personal, professional, and fully transparent, covering everything from university selection and documentation preparation to visa guidance and post-arrival support. CraftEd operates through The Craft Catalyst's Kenya office on Riverside Drive, Nairobi, and through their Malaysia partner office at D'Sara Sentral, Shah Alam, Selangor.

## Project Summary

> **Objective:** Build a dedicated website for CraftEd that serves as the primary digital touchpoint for East African students and families exploring university education in Malaysia — featuring informative content, consultation booking, webinar registration, an eligibility checker, and a news blog that establishes thought leadership in the African-to-Malaysia education pathway.

## Challenges

- University placement is a **high-trust, high-consideration decision** for families — the site needed to build immediate credibility through professional design, CUE accreditation prominence, and clear communication of the placement process
- Had to address the **specific concerns of parents and students** considering studying abroad: Is Malaysia safe? What are the costs? Which universities are available? How does the process work?
- Needed a **content-driven strategy** to attract organic traffic from students and parents searching for study-abroad options — this required a blog with SEO-optimized articles
- Required **multiple conversion pathways**: schedule a consultation, register for a webinar, check eligibility, or simply make contact — each serving a different stage of the user's decision journey
- The service operates across **two countries** (Kenya and Malaysia) with different contact numbers, currencies, and time zones — the site needed to present this clearly without creating confusion
- Had to differentiate CraftEd from **generic study-abroad agencies** by emphasizing their CUE approval, personalized approach, and exclusive focus on the Malaysia pathway

## My Role & Responsibilities

As the **lead web developer and designer**, I was responsible for:

## Key Features Implemented

### Homepage & Value Proposition

The CraftEd homepage immediately establishes credibility with the **CUE-approved University Agent** designation — prominently displayed to reassure parents and students that this is a vetted, government-recognized service. The core value proposition is clear: CraftEd supports students and families from the first consultation to successful university placement and settling into life in Malaysia. Three key features are highlighted with numbered presentations: **Global Education Pathways** (access to top Malaysian institutions), **CUE-Approved University Agency** (Commission for University Education accreditation), and **End-to-End Admissions Support** (from university selection and documentation prep to visa guidance). Each feature is designed to answer a specific concern a parent or student would have, creating a comprehensive but not overwhelming overview of the service.

### News & Insights Blog

The blog at **crafted.thecraftcatalyst.com/category/news/** serves as CraftEd's content marketing engine, attracting organic traffic from students and parents researching study-abroad options. Articles cover topics like "What Two Decades in African Talent Has Taught Me About Preparing Our Children," "10 Things That Will Make You Say: Wait — Malaysia Did That?", "Far From Home, Yet Somehow Familiar," and "What If You Could Know Your Child's Perfect Career Path — Before They Apply?" These articles are strategically written to target long-tail search queries while building thought leadership and trust. The blog uses a clean, readable layout with prominent "Read more" CTAs that keep users engaged and moving deeper into the site.

### Webinar Registration & Consultation Booking

The site provides **two distinct conversion pathways** designed for users at different stages of their decision journey. The **webinar registration page** captures first name, last name, email, and phone number — a low-commitment entry point for students who are curious but not yet ready for a one-on-one conversation. The **consultation scheduling form** is more detailed, including a dropdown to select the type of help needed: Career Path & Advice, University Placement, General Inquiry, or Other, along with an open message field. This dual-pathway approach ensures the site captures leads at every stage, from casual interest to serious intent.

### "What Happens Next?" User Journey

A dedicated section walks potential students through the **three-step process**: (1) We schedule a call at your convenience, (2) A personalized consultation to understand your goals, (3) We match you with the right institution or programme. This transparency about the process is critical for a service that involves significant financial and life decisions — parents and students need to know exactly what they're signing up for before they share their personal information. The section reduces anxiety and friction, making it easier for users to take the first step.

### Dual-Office Contact & Operating Hours

The contact section displays **comprehensive details for both offices**: The Craft Catalyst Ltd. on Riverside Drive, Nairobi, Kenya (+254 742 950 093) and the Malaysia partner office at D'Sara Sentral, Shah Alam, Selangor (+60 36151 6403). Operating hours are clearly stated (Monday to Friday: 9:00 am to 5:00 pm, Saturdays: 9:00 am to 1:00 pm). This dual-office presentation signals to families that CraftEd has genuine, on-the-ground presence in both the origin and destination countries — not just a website operating from one location. A privacy policy consent checkbox on the consultation form ensures Kenya DPA 2019 compliance.

The CraftEd website has become a key driver of The Craft Catalyst's university placement business:

- Attracted **organic search traffic** through the SEO-optimized blog, targeting high-intent queries from students researching study-in-Malaysia options

- Strengthened the organization's positioning as a **specialist in the Africa-to-Malaysia education corridor** — a niche with growing demand but limited dedicated online resources
