# Case Study: Rafiki Art Space – Empowering Youth Through a Digital Platform for Art & Community

## Client: Rafiki Art Space (Kibera, Nairobi, Kenya)

Rafiki Art Space is a non-profit community art center located in the heart of Kibera, Nairobi's largest informal settlement. Founded by Clinton Artisto — a self-taught composite realism artist who grew up in Kibera and began painting at age 7 to fund his own education — the organization is dedicated to lifting children and youth out of poverty through the transformative power of art, education, and creative mentorship. The space offers art workshops, after-school tutoring, exhibitions, a volunteer residency programme, and a unique Kibera Art Walk experience, all aimed at fostering creativity, confidence, and opportunity in one of East Africa's most vibrant yet underserved communities.

## Project Summary

> **Objective:** Build a modern, visually compelling website that serves as Rafiki Art Space's digital home — enabling online donations, artwork sales through an e-commerce shop, workshop and gallery showcases, volunteer residency applications, and the Kibera Art Walk booking system, while amplifying their mission to a global audience.

## Challenges

- No existing website or digital presence to showcase the organization's work and mission
- Need to integrate donation functionality (GiveWP) alongside a WooCommerce-powered art shop
- Required a booking system for the Kibera Art Walk — an immersive, guided community art tour
- Must represent a non-profit with a compelling visual identity that reflects African art, culture, and community
- Needed to highlight multiple program areas: workshops, gallery, residency, impact, and an online shop
- Limited budget typical of non-profit organizations, requiring a cost-effective but scalable solution
- Must be easy for the client's team to manage and update content independently

## Key Features Implemented

### Non-Profit Donation System

The site integrates **GiveWP** — a leading WordPress donation plugin — enabling Rafiki Art Space to accept online donations, run fundraising campaigns with goal tracking and progress bars, and display donor activity. The donation system is seamlessly woven into the site's navigation and footer, ensuring visitors can contribute at any point during their browsing experience. Campaign-specific pages showcase fundraising goals, donor comments, and progress statistics, building trust and transparency with supporters.

### E-Commerce Art Shop

A full **WooCommerce**-powered online shop was set up with three main product categories — **Merchandise**, **Paintings**, and **Recycled Art** — allowing Rafiki Art Space to sell artwork and branded merchandise globally. The shop includes a responsive cart experience, secure checkout, and a product gallery that beautifully presents each piece. Revenue from art sales directly supports the organization's programs and the artists who create the work.

### Kibera Art Walk Booking System

The **Kibera Art Walk** is one of Rafiki's signature experiences — an immersive guided tour through Kibera's vibrant street art scene led by Clinton Artisto and the Rafiki Collective. I built a dedicated booking page with a custom booking plugin that captures the visitor's full name, email, phone number, preferred event date, time, and additional details. The page also showcases gallery images from past walks and clearly communicates that proceeds support Rafiki Art Space and local artisans.

### Workshop & Program Showcase

The **Workshops page** highlights the breadth of Rafiki's community programs, including hands-on painting sessions, photography and film production, modern dance and music, fashion shows, recycled art initiatives, and mural installations. Each workshop is presented with a description that communicates both the creative activity and its broader social impact, helping volunteers, donors, and participants understand the depth of programming offered.

### Volunteer Residency Programme Page

The **Residency page** communicates Rafiki's artist residency and volunteer programme, inviting local and international artists, creatives, and institutions to collaborate. The page outlines the residency's goals — strengthening existing workshops, creating a robust activities programme, and fostering inter-generational cultural exchange — while providing clear calls to action for prospective volunteers and partners.

### Gallery & Visual Storytelling

The site features a dedicated **Gallery** section anchored by the Kibera Art Walk experience. Vibrant imagery of community murals, local artisan workshops, dance and music performances, and street art is woven throughout the site, creating a rich visual narrative that draws visitors into the world of Kibera's creative community.

The website has transformed Rafiki Art Space's ability to connect with the world:

- Established a **professional digital presence** for a grassroots non-profit that previously had none
